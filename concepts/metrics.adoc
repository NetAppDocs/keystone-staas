= Keystone STaaS storage metrics and definitions
:hardbreaks:
:nofooter:
:icons: font
:linkattrs:
:imagesdir: ../media/

[.lead]
Keystone STaaS uses specific terms to measure and report on your storage subscription. Understanding these terms helps you interpret dashboard data and subscription reports.

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
| Term | Definition

| Capacity (GiB, TiB, PiB)
| Data storage capacity is measured using powers of 1024: 1 GiB = 1024^3^ bytes, 1 TiB = 1024^4^ bytes, and 1 PiB = 1024^5^ bytes.

| IOPS
| IOPS (I/O operations per second) is the number of protocol operations per second requested by the application. IOPS is displayed in the operations counter chart on the Keystone dashboard.

| Availability
| Availability is the percentage of I/O requests successfully responded to by the service out of the total I/O requests made in a month. This is measured at the service demarcation in a month and does not include the scheduled service downtime or unavailability of the facilities, network, or other services provided by the customer.

| Durability
| Durability is the percentage of data that can be accessed without loss of fidelity, excluding customer-caused deletion or corruption.

| Latency
| Latency is the time to service an I/O request received from a client, measured at the service demarcation point (storage controller I/O port).
|===
