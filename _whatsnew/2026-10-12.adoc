This Keystone STaaS release includes the following updates in the Keystone dashboard in the NetApp Console.

=== Generate health insights for Keystone subscriptions
You can download on-demand PowerPoint reports or schedule one-time, weekly, monthly, or quarterly delivery from *Storage > Keystone > Monitoring > Health reports* in the NetApp Console. Each report combines subscription usage, operational health, risks, and vulnerabilities across selected subscriptions, helping you identify concerns and share actionable insights with recipients. To learn more, refer to https://docs.netapp.com/us-en/keystone-staas/integrations/health-assessment-reports.html[Generate and schedule health reports for Keystone subscriptions].

=== View ONTAP cluster and aggregate capacity
You can view physical capacity, logical capacity, and storage efficiency for ONTAP clusters and their aggregates in *Assets > Clusters and aggregates*. This is available only for Keystone STaaS subscriptions billed on physical capacity. To learn more, refer to https://docs.netapp.com/us-en/keystone-staas/integrations/assets.html[View assets across your Keystone subscriptions] and https://docs.netapp.com/us-en/keystone-staas/integrations/assets-tab.html[View assets associated with a Keystone subscription].
